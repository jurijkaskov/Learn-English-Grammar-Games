## 1. Character Purpose & Narrative Role

The green dragon mascot is the emotional heart, supportive guide, and constant learning companion of **Learn English: Grammar Games**.

His core persona is:
```text
learning companion
+
friendly guide
+
adventure partner
+
progress mascot
```

### What He IS:
- **Curious**: Intrigued by words, grammar puzzles, and sentence structures.
- **Friendly & Approachable**: Warm, gentle, and eager to cheer for every milestone.
- **Smart & Attentive**: Loves reading his little purple grammar book and noting tricky patterns.
- **Playful & Energetic**: Bounces happily during streaks and jumps in celebration.
- **Supportive**: Never scolds or punishes mistakes; offers a thoughtful nod and an encouraging gesture.
- **Adventurous**: Equipped with his travel backpack, exploring the kingdoms of the Grammar Journey.

### What He IS NOT:
- ❌ **Not a School Teacher**: No suit, no glasses perched on a long nose, no pointer stick at a blackboard, no lecturing demeanor.
- ❌ **Not a Strict Instructor**: Never condescending, critical, or disciplinary.
- ❌ **Not an Infant / Baby**: Not a helpless chibi toddler in diapers or with giant pacifiers.
- ❌ **Not a Ferocious Battle Monster**: No sharp scales, no fiery breath of destruction, no predatory claws, no scary spikes.
- ❌ **Not an Alien / Sci-Fi Creature**: Whimsical cartoon fantasy creature grounded in fairy-tale warmth.

## 2. Perceived Age & Personality Archetype

- **Perceived Age**: **Young adventurous fantasy dragon** (the creature equivalent of an eager 10–12-year-old explorer).
- **Universal Appeal**: He feels playful enough for children (ages 8+), yet clean, well-proportioned, and design-minded enough that adult learners never feel infantilized or embarrassed to have him on screen.
- **Companion Bond**: He is learning *with* the user, sharing the joy of unlocking lessons and conquering grammar bosses together.

## 5. Construction Proportions & Proportional Grid

The character uses a strict, reproducible **8-Unit Construction Grid**:

| Body Region | Proportion of Total Height | Equivalent Grid Units | Key Relationships |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Head (including horns)** | **42%** | 3.36 / 8.0 units | Head Width ≈ 1.15 × Torso Width |
| **Torso & Neck** | **28%** | 2.24 / 8.0 units | Compact pear; chest slightly broader than waist |
| **Legs** | **18%** | 1.44 / 8.0 units | Short, grounded, rounded columns |
| **Feet** | **12%** | 0.96 / 8.0 units | Slightly oversized for stability & grounding |
| **Total Height** | **100%** | **8.0 units** | Ground to horn tip |

- **Width-to-Height Ratio**: In neutral standing pose, overall bounding box width (including tail curve) is **0.72 × total height**.
- **Tail Length**: Approximately **55%–65%** of total character height, flowing out smoothly from the lower spine.

## 6. Head & Snout Anatomy

- **Head Shape**: Oversized, soft rounded pear (slightly wider across the cheeks than at the crown). No sharp reptilian corners or angular jawlines.
- **Front View**: Soft chubby oval / rounded balloon shape.
- **Profile / 3/4 View**: The snout protrudes softly forward by roughly **20%–25%** of head width. It is short, broad, and blunt like a cute cartoon dinosaur or friendly salamander, NOT an elongated crocodilian snout.
- **Cheeks**: Plump and rounded, lifting upward slightly when smiling.
- **Nostrils**: Two tiny, subtle soft-dark dots on the upper snout. Zero detailed cavities, skin creases, or flaring nostrils.
- **Mouth**: Gentle curved line with small rounded upturns at the corners. When open in a wide smile, displays warm coral mouth interior and optionally **1–2 tiny rounded milk teeth** at the top. **Strictly NO sharp fangs or predatory teeth.**

## 7. Eyes, Eyebrows & Gaze

The eyes are the primary vehicle of emotional communication and empathy.

### Eye Construction:
- **Shape**: Large rounded upright ovals.
- **Placement**: Situated slightly above the horizontal centerline of the head, giving a youthful, open-minded expression.
- **Size**: Eye height ≈ **22%–25%** of head height.
- **Inter-ocular Distance**: Spaced by approximately **0.4 × eye width**.

### Eye Layers (Inner to Outer):
1. **Sclera (Eye White)**: Warm soft cream white (`#FDFCF7`), never harsh artificial `#FFFFFF`.
2. **Iris**: Distinct two-tone warm palette contrasting the green body:
   - Outer Iris: Deep warm espresso brown (`#4E342E`).
   - Inner Iris: Warm amber flame (`#D35400`).
3. **Pupil**: Large rounded charcoal-black oval (`#261815`). **STRICTLY FORBIDDEN**: snake slits, aggressive cat slits, or robotic circles.
4. **Specular Highlights**:
   - Primary: Crisp white circle/pill located top-left (10 o'clock position), occupying ~20% of pupil area.
   - Secondary (optional): Tiny white pinprick highlight at 4 o'clock for extra sparkle in happy/reward states.

### Eyebrows:
- Floating cartoon expression strokes above the eyes matching the dark body shadow tone (`#3FAE55` or `#2E9E5B`).
- Highly dynamic: curved upward for happy/curious, tilted inward for thinking/focused, gently raised for surprise, slightly lowered for mild disappointment.

---

## 8. Horns

- **Quantity**: Exactly **two (2)** symmetrical horns. Never one, never three, never antler branches.
- **Placement**: Upper posterior curve of the cranium, pointing upward and arching gently backward.
- **Size**: Horn height ≈ **12%–15%** of head height.
- **Shape**: Soft, stubby, rounded conical nubs. The tips are gently rounded, never razor-sharp or menacing.
- **Colour**: Warm buttery cream (`#F5DDA6`) with soft golden-beige base shading (`#DEC388`).

---

## 9. Torso, Belly & Dorsal Elements

- **Torso**: Compact pear silhouette. Tapers slightly toward the neck, with a soft rounded belly resting comfortably over the thighs.
- **Ventral Belly Patch**:
  - Shape: Smooth vertical oval occupying the central 60% of the chest and abdomen.
  - Colour: Pale fresh mint (`#CFF1C5`), providing a luminous, clean contrast against the richer emerald green body.
- **Dorsal Accents**:
  - Exactly **3 to 4 tiny rounded bumps/ridges** along the upper spine and base of the neck.
  - Soft mint green (`#77DA83`). Never jagged sharp spikes or saw blades.

---

## 10. Arms, Hands & Feet

- **Arms**: Short, soft, and flexible cartoon limbs. Designed with enough reach and articulation to point at UI elements, hold an open book, scratch the chin in thought, or raise both arms in victory.
- **Hands**: Simplified cartoon hands featuring **three (3) rounded fingers** and a soft thumb pad.
  - Zero sharp claws. Tiny pale cream rounded nail tips (`#FFFDE7`) are optional but must be soft and blunt.
- **Legs**: Short, sturdy, rounded columns providing clear visual balance and grounding.
- **Feet**: Slightly oversized rounded pads with **three (3) rounded toes**, ensuring stable footing in standing, running, and jumping poses.

---

## 11. Tail

- **Structure**: Smooth, flexible, and muscular taper extending from the base of the spine.
- **Length**: Approximately **55%–65%** of the character’s total standing height.
- **Shape**: Thick and rounded at the base, tapering smoothly to a soft rounded tip.
- **Action & Pose Role**:
  - Serves as a dynamic visual counterweight in action poses (curling upward in joy, flowing backward when running, resting calmly on the floor when idle/reading).
  - No spikes, mace balls, or fire at the tip in the base design.

---

## 12. Wings

- **Structure**: Two small, rounded cartoon dragon wings attached to the upper back.
- **Size**: Each wing is approximately **25%–30%** of torso height. Intentionally undersized so the character looks like a young explorer whose wings are for gliding/hopping, not a massive flying war beast.
- **Membrane**: 2 to 3 soft scalloped sections with rounded tips. No sharp bat claw at the joint elbow.
- **Idle State**: Neatly folded against the back, peeking gently over the shoulders without cluttering the silhouette.
- **Expressive States**:
  - *Celebrating*: Flapped wide open with excitement.
  - *Jumping / Running*: Angled outward for aerodynamic balance.
  - *Sleeping*: Relaxed and draped gently against the side.
- **Colour**: Medium spring green (`#4AB75E`), slightly deeper than the base skin to preserve clean depth separation.

## 15. Lighting, Shading & Outline Rules

- **Lighting Direction**: Consistent **soft ambient light from the upper-left (10 o'clock)**.
- **Highlights**: Soft, subtle oval/pill glints on top surfaces (top of the head, nose bridge, top of the backpack, curve of the horns). Opacity 25%–40%.
- **Shading Treatment**: Soft self-shading following rounded volumes. No harsh, sharp cel-shading creases; no noisy ambient occlusion or photorealistic texture bakes.
- **Outline Treatment**: **ZERO black outlines**. Edges are established by contrasting color blocks or by delicate 1.5dp–2.5dp tinted outlines matching the local shadow color (`#3FAE55`).
- **Contact Shadow**: Soft horizontal oval (`rgba(30,41,59,0.18)`) under the feet when standing on a ground plane.

## 17. Facial Expression System

Expressions are communicated through subtle, appealing shifts in eyebrows, eyelids, pupils, and mouth lines—**without distorting core cranial anatomy**:

```text
┌──────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┬──────────────────────┐
│ Expression       │ Eyebrows             │ Eyes                 │ Mouth                │
├──────────────────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┼──────────────────────┤
│ **Neutral**      │ Relaxed gentle curve │ Open, round pupils   │ Small closed smile   │
│ **Happy**        │ High arched, bright  │ Cheerful upturned crescents| Open friendly smile│
│ **Very Happy**   │ Raised high in joy   │ Sparkle glint, crescents| Wide open laugh (teeth)|
│ **Thinking**     │ One raised, one flat │ Pupils look up-left  │ Small puckered corner│
│ **Surprised**    │ High raised arches   │ Wide circular irises │ Small rounded "O"    │
│ **Confused**     │ Asymmetric cocked    │ One wide, one squint │ Wavy questioning line│
│ **Mild Disappoint**| Gently sloped down │ Soft drooped eyelids │ Small closed flat line│
│ **Encouraging**  │ Confident firm arch  │ Focused, warm        │ Determined smile     │
│ **Proud**        │ Relaxed high         │ Half-closed confident│ Smug joyful grin     │
│ **Focused**      │ Straight angled down │ Narrowed on target   │ Tight determined line│
│ **Sleeping**     │ Relaxed flat         │ Closed curved lines  │ Tiny peaceful smile  │
└──────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┴──────────────────────┘
```

> **Pedagogical Empathy Rule**: In `Mild Disappointed` (wrong answer), the character **NEVER** cries, sheds tears, or looks broken-hearted. He looks thoughtfully puzzled or ready to try again, communicating: *"Not quite! Let's examine the rule together."*

## 20. Character Customization & Cosmetic Anchor Zones

To ensure future customization (hats, outfits, backpacks, accessories) does not break character anatomy, **six (6) standardized Anchor Zones** are defined:

```text
               [ HEAD ANCHOR ] ──── Crowns, scholar caps, explorer hats
               [ FACE ANCHOR ] ──── Reading glasses, detective monocles
               [ NECK ANCHOR ] ──── Scarves, bowties, explorer medals
               [ TORSO ANCHOR ] ─── Waistcoats, explorer vests, robes
               [ BACK ANCHOR ] ──── Alternative backpacks, magic banners
               [ HAND ANCHOR ] ──── Books, pencils, wands, shields
```

### Cosmetic Design Rules:
1. **Horn Passthrough**: All hats and crowns must feature subtle cutouts or sit naturally between the two horns. Horns are NEVER magically deleted.
2. **Backpack Swap**: The base purple backpack is a default cosmetic item. New backpacks occupy the exact same mid-back anchor zone below the wings.
3. **Preserved Anatomy**: Custom outfits may drape over the torso, but the green skin tone, tail, and facial anatomy remain 100% consistent.

## 21. Animation Readiness & Rigging Regions

```text
Hierarchy Tree:

├── Torso
│   ├── Belly Plate (deformable mesh)
│   ├── Scarf (secondary drag motion)
│   ├── Left Arm ── Left Hand (3 fingers)
│   ├── Right Arm ── Right Hand (3 fingers)
│   ├── Left Wing (2-bone chain, flap cycle)
│   ├── Right Wing (2-bone chain, flap cycle)
│   ├── Backpack (secondary bounce)
│   ├── Tail (3-bone smooth spline chain)
│   └── Head
│       ├── Left Horn / Right Horn
│       ├── Snout & Mouth (morph targets)
│       └── Eyes (eyelid blinks, pupil track)
└── Legs
    ├── Left Leg ── Left Foot (3 toes)
```

## 26. Accessibility & Pedagogical Independence

- **Supportive, Not Essential**: The dragon provides warmth, delight, and feedback reinforcement. However, critical educational information (correctness, scores, grammar explanations) **MUST NEVER** rely solely on the mascot’s visual expression.
- **Screen Reader Semantics**: Decorative instances of the mascot must supply `contentDescription = null` to avoid polluting TalkBack streams. Significant feedback states (e.g. results celebration) provide clear semantic text in the accompanying Compose cards.
